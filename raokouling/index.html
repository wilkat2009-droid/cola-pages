TONGUE TWISTERS

绕口令三段

①
载入中
Māma qí mǎ.
妈妈骑马。
Mom rides a horse.
Mǎ màn, māma mà mǎ.
马慢，妈妈骂马。
The horse is slow — Mom scolds it.
②
载入中
Nèiwài yǒu sìshísì zhī shīzi,
内外有四十四只狮子，
Inside and outside there are forty-four lions.
bùzhī shì sìshísì zhī sǐ shīzi,
不知是四十四只死狮子，
Can't tell whether they're forty-four dead lions,
háishì sìshísì zhī shí shīzi.
还是四十四只石狮子。
or forty-four stone lions.
③
载入中
Bābǎi biāobīng bēn běipō,
八百标兵奔北坡，
Eight hundred front-line troops dash for the north slope,
pàobīng bìngpái běibiān pǎo.
炮兵并排北边跑。
the artillery runs abreast along the north side.
Pàobīng pà bǎ biāobīng pèng,
炮兵怕把标兵碰，
The artillery fear bumping the troops;
biāobīng pà pèng pàobīng pào.
标兵怕碰炮兵炮。
the troops fear bumping the artillery's guns.
练什么：① m（妈妈／马／骂）　② s 和 sh（四、死、石）　③ b 和 p（标兵／炮兵）
