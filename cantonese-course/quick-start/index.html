A note about festivals & the Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance

Mid-Autumn Festival is a time for family, moon-viewing and community traditions. The Tai Hang Fire Dragon Dance is a local community tradition; learn about it respectfully, and follow the organisers' rules. Never enter the performance route or touch equipment.
